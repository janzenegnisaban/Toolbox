Process

From walkthrough to handover

A simple, documented process so you know what will be installed, where it will sit, and how to use it.

How a Toolbox install works

Four clear stages from first call to finished system.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We discuss your priorities—entrances, stock, parking, or after-hours activity—and gather site photos or book a visit.

  2. 02

    Walkthrough & plan

    On site we map doors, aisles, and blind spots, then recommend camera count, heights, and recording storage.

  3. 03

    Install day

    Clean mounting, tidy cabling, network connection, and NVR setup—tested live before we pack up.

  4. 04

    Handover & support

    You get live views, playback basics, camera labels, and aftercare options for upgrades or service.
